OldOS YouTube v13 / Camera V60.3.44 — capture callback repair v3

Observed failure: photo iris receives AVCapturePhotoOutput willCapture, but
stays closed and no image bounces into the well. In v2, both reopen and well
were exclusively dependent on didFinishProcessingPhoto callback, while capture
delegate cleanup waited for Photo Library authorization and saving.

v3 changes:
- The terminal AVCapturePhotoOutput didFinishCapture callback always completes
  the shutter animation (idempotently), including error/missing-data paths.
- Photo Library saving occurs independently and can never delay restoring
  the shutter or releasing the in-progress capture delegate.
- Each capture is keyed by its actual AVCapturePhotoSettings.uniqueID. A 6s
  watchdog releases an uncompleted shutter with an explicit error; stale photo
  callbacks cannot affect the next shot.
- A delayed valid image is still animated into the thumbnail well after the
  iris has reopened, rather than being silently discarded.
- Capture callback logs distinguish no capture vs no photo data vs no save.
- Concurrent shutter presses are suppressed until the delegate returns.
- YouTube v13 implementation and flip safety preserved unchanged.

On-device result is NOT verified; only source checks are possible on Linux.
On an iPhone use Console.app / Xcode output and filter for 'CAMERA PHOTO'.
